## Featured Educational Project

### Prompting Success with Generative AI in Teaching & Student Research

*A dynamic, hands-on workshop designed to equip ACS faculty with the tools, strategies, and confidence to use generative AI to support student success.*

This repository highlights the Prompting Success with Generative AI in Teaching & Student Research workshop developed for the Associated Colleges of the South (ACS). The workshop introduces faculty to prompt engineering as a strategy for using AI to strengthen teaching, learning, assessment, and student research while preserving student agency and critical thinking.

[View the archived workshop materials in the ACS Resource Library](https://www.acsouth.edu/resources/prompting-success-with-generative-ai-in-teaching-and-student-research/)

Funded by the Associated Colleges of the South.
